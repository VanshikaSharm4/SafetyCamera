Criminal Recognition from Police Database

Detects if a known criminal has entered the premises. Facial data (shared or approved by law enforcement) will be used to create or access a custom dataset, enabling real-time alerts when matches occur.

Fall Detection

Uses computer vision to detect sudden human falls, especially useful in shops, elderly-care areas, and warehouses. It immediately alerts staff or emergency services if a fall is detected.
